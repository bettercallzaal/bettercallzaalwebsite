The through-line

Every venture points the same way: give creators the ownership, upside, and infrastructure that extractive platforms take - and build it all in public.

Zaal trained as an electrical engineer at RIT and spent his early career in automation, leading a $1.5M robotics project before moving to building automation at The Jackson Laboratory in Maine. That systems discipline is the foundation: real, running systems, not slideware. In 2023 he founded The ZAO to flip the math for independent artists - and it grew into governance, on-chain markets, metaverse concerts, festivals, and an open-source fleet.

Questions

Who is BetterCallZaal?

BetterCallZaal is Zaal Panthaki, an electrical engineer and open-source builder based in Maine. He is the founder of The ZAO, a decentralized creator ecosystem, and the co-founder of WaveWarZ - building AI-agent infrastructure and creator tools in public across 80+ repositories.

What is The ZAO?

The ZAO is a decentralized impact community for independent music artists, founded by Zaal Panthaki. It returns profit margin, data, and IP ownership to creators, running on Farcaster and Base with weekly peer-validated Fractal governance.

What does Zaal build?

Creator infrastructure in the open: WaveWarZ (on-chain music markets on Solana), COC Concertz (metaverse concerts), ZABAL (ecosystem token and builder program), Sparkz, zaalcaster, and BCZ Strategies, an open-source consulting practice.

What is Zaal's background?

Zaal is an electrical engineer (B.S., RIT, 2022). He led a $1.5M robotics project at PCC Structurals and works in building automation at The Jackson Laboratory in Maine - the systems discipline behind everything he ships.
