# 10 — ZAOstock Sponsorship Playbook

> **Status:** Research complete
> **Date:** 2026-08-15
> **Goal:** How to package, price, and pitch ZAOstock (Oct 3, 2026, Ellsworth, Maine - year-one, free-to-attend, one stage, part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth) to sponsors - and what belongs in the deck. Sponsors are the site's stated #1 audience; the festival is ~7 weeks out.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Audience fit beats headcount.** 2026 sponsorship research is explicit: a small event whose crowd exactly matches a brand's target is worth more than a big diluted one. ZAOstock's pitch is not "thousands of people" - it is *the* arts-community moment in a town adjacent to Acadia's 4M+ annual visitors, stacked on an established 9-year-old event (Art of Ellsworth) that de-risks year one.
- **Local businesses are the realistic buyers** - regional/local sponsorship expanded in 2026 because brands want authentic community connection and smaller checks are easier to justify. Target Ellsworth/MDI-corridor businesses first (restaurants, breweries, outfitters, banks/credit unions, realtors, hospitality), then Maine-wide brands, then web3-native money as a separate lane.
- **Sponsors in 2026 buy outcomes, not logos.** Decks that win connect tiers to data (attendance counts, social impressions, post-event report) and offer **utility-shaped activations** (charging lounge, water station, kids zone) over banner placement - activations deliver 3-5x the brand recall of passive signage.
- **Zaal has an unusual differentiator: onchain receipts.** Nobody else pitching a Maine community festival can offer a post-event report with verifiable numbers (onchain artist payouts, WaveWarZ-style live dashboards) plus a professionally-produced media recap. Lead with transparency as a feature.

---

## 1. Tier structure (scaled for a free, first-year, small-town festival)

Standard practice is Bronze->Platinum with each tier adding data access and activation depth. Scaled to Ellsworth reality (checks local businesses can justify):

| Tier | Suggested band | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| Friend of the Fest | $250-500 | Name on site + thank-you cast/post + program listing |
| Bronze | $500-1,000 | Above + logo on stage banner + social shoutout during the day |
| Silver | $1,500-2,500 | Above + a utility activation (water station, charging table, merch table adjacency) + inclusion in the recap video |
| Gold / Presenting partner | $3,500-7,500 (1-2 slots max) | "Presented with X" placement, stage-mention cadence, booth/lounge, first right of renewal for year two, full post-event data report |
| In-kind lane | value-priced | Sound/staging, food, lodging for artists, print - priced at equivalent tier |

Notes from the research: keep tiers few and *tailor per sponsor* (2026 best practice is moving away from rigid one-size tiers); anchor pricing to what the sponsor gets per real attendee contact, not CPM fantasies; a 1-2 slot cap on the top tier creates scarcity and protects the community feel of a free event.

## 2. What goes in the deck (order matters)

1. **One-line story + date/place** - why ZAOstock exists (real festival, not a web3 conference; artists own the outcome).
2. **The audience** - Ellsworth + MDI corridor, Art of Ellsworth's 9-year track record, Acadia adjacency (4M+ visitors annually), plus Zaal's digital reach (1,000+ across Farcaster/Discord, 400+ newsletter editions) as the amplification layer.
3. **Proof** - 2 festivals already produced (ZAO-PALOOZA NYC, ZAO-CHELLA Miami), WaveWarZ onchain numbers, photos of past events (the media gap bites here - prioritize getting NYC/Miami photos into the deck).
4. **Tiers + activations menu** - lead each tier with the activation, not the logo. Frame as "improve the attendee's day with your name on the improvement."
5. **The transparency promise** - post-event report within 14 days: attendance estimate, social metrics, recap video, and onchain-verifiable artist payout receipts. No other local event offers this.
6. **Ask + contact** - one CTA, one person (Zaal), phone/email/Telegram bot.

Format: PDF + a `/zaostock/sponsors` page mirroring it (the deck research is unanimous that decks die in inboxes; a link that renders on a phone gets forwarded). Match the ZAOstock visual identity, high-res photos over stock.

## 3. Outreach mechanics (7 weeks out)

- Week 1-2: warm local list (businesses Zaal/the Art of Ellsworth network already knows), in-person or call first, deck as follow-up. Local sponsorship closes on relationships, not cold email.
- Week 3-4: Maine-regional brands + banks/credit unions (community-sponsorship budgets are annual and often still open in Q3), plus in-kind lane (sound, print, food).
- Ongoing: web3-native lane (Base ecosystem, music-web3 projects) pitched separately with the onchain-receipts angle - do not mix the two decks' languages.
- Every "no" gets asked for the year-two right of first refusal list - year-one festivals sell year two during year one.
